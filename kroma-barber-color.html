One full session, every booking.

No rushed cuts. Whatever you book, you get the same four steps with the same barber.

  1. Consultation

    We look at your growth, your crown and how you style it at home before anything gets cut.

  2. Wash

    A quick wash so the cut is done on clean, natural-fall hair.

  3. Cut

    Clipper, scissor or razor, whatever the shape needs. No watching the clock.

  4. Finish

    Line-up, styling, and a quick lesson so you can get the same look on day three.
