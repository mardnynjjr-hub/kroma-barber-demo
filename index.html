KROMA Barber Co. — 3 Design Directions

Three takes on the homepage for a booking-first barbershop in Sihanoukville. They share the same content, booking form and accessibility fixes. Only the look and the motion change.

  • Monochrome

    kroma-barber-demo.html

    Black, white and grey only. The hero shows a skin fade as six grey bands, one for each clipper guard.

    • Archivo, set narrow for headings
    • Inverted black booking section
    • One load animation, nothing else moves

    Open monochrome

  • Colour blocking

    kroma-barber-color.html

    Klein blue for the big blocks, lemon yellow for buttons and accents. Fade bars are drawn to scale, 12 mm down to skin.

    • Blue #002FA7, yellow #FFE600
    • Yellow only sits on blue or near-black
    • No gradients

    Open colour blocking

  • Motion

    kroma-barber-motion.html

    The colour-blocked page with slow, weighted motion: a curtain opening, headings revealed line by line, and a pinned process section.

    • CSS and IntersectionObserver, no library
    • Lighter parallax, no pinning on phones
    • Static for reduced-motion settings

    Open motion
