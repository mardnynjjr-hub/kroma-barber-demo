Built around the chair, not the counter.

KROMA opened with one rule: no rushed cuts. Every booking gets a full session — consultation, wash, cut, and a finish that actually holds past day three.

We run three chairs, not ten, so the standard never drops to fill more seats. If a barber's booked, you wait for them — not for whoever's free.
